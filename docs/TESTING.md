### Acoustic echo cancellation (#114)

- **Offline, deterministic (`echo::tests`, controller reworked in #83):** a speech-like noise far end is convolved with a 100 ms decaying room response (40 ms bulk delay) and fed through the canceller in 10 ms blocks. After 8 s the echo return loss enhancement over the last 2 s is 38.6 dB (floor 25 dB). With a near-end talker between 7 s and 9 s, the talker is kept (residual 15.1 dB below it, floor 8 dB), the echo stays 31.0 dB down (floor 10 dB) and cancellation recovers to 42.2 dB afterwards (floor 20 dB). A silent reference passes the microphone through bit-for-bit within 1e-6; the FFT round-trips; queues and filter memory are bounded; the playback tap releases only what the device consumed and pads idle time with silence. After a mute or push-to-talk gap that moves the reference pairing by 8 ms, `path_changed` restores 20 dB within five seconds (4 dB at second two, 30 dB at second six) where without it the filter stays at 6 dB or less for the six seconds measured.

### Noise suppression: High quality (milestone 23a)

- **Quality metric:** `high_quality_beats_light_on_keyboard_and_background_voice` on `keyboard-voice.wav` (keyboard clicks, a distant reverberant talker and room tone): noisy 4.00 dB, Light 7.71 dB (+3.71), High quality 14.28 dB (+10.28), so High quality leads by **+6.56 dB SI-SDR** (floor +3.5) and by **+20.8 dB** attenuation of speech-absent windows (Light 5.4, High quality 26.2; floor +10); output peaks 0.287 and 0.227 against an input peak of 0.416. The talker needs steady room noise to survive (near-silent room tone makes DeepFilterNet attenuate this synthetic voice), which is why the fixture carries -40 dBFS pink room tone.
- **Behavior tests (no private state):** with a stand-in engine behind the real worker thread, selecting High quality returns at once while its build is blocked and every block is still answered; the hand-over repeats only the extra delay (3 blocks plus at most the queue depth) and never skips speech; switching back resumes exactly where the slower engine stopped; the runtime is dropped on its own thread on switching away and when the call ends; a failed build keeps the current engine and reselecting retries; a worker that cannot keep up, slows down or panics hands the stream back to Light with an error without stalling the caller; blocks in flight at a mute never leak after it; fast toggling never builds two runtimes at once. Engine-level tests step fake devices: capture frames stay contiguous and playback keeps decoding while the build is gated, across the hand-over and the switch away; muting with High quality active runs no inference and leaves the runtime loaded. The real model hands over on the real worker at its published 1,440-sample latency.
- **Cost (release, same protocol):** build 95-120 ms on its own thread; `process()` per 10 ms block mean 0.21-0.23 ms, p50 0.12-0.33, p95 0.35-0.36, p99 0.38-0.42, max 0.69-0.95 ms (2.1-2.3 % of one core back to back; SPEC estimate 5-15 %, p95 target < 0.5 ms). At a real-time cadence the worker is busy 4.5-4.8 % of one core while the owner's `push` + `pop` costs a mean 6.3-6.7 us (p95 8.8-9.7 us, max 57 us), less than Light because inference is off the owner; `select()` returned in 50-65 us and the model took over after 140-180 ms of real-time audio. A block's round trip through the worker (queueing plus inference) is p50 0.53 ms, p95 1.06 ms, max 3.3 ms.
- **Added latency:** 1,440 samples = **30 ms** algorithmic (best-alignment lag 1,440), plus about 1 ms worker round trip and at most one 5 ms owner tick, so 31-36 ms against Light's 10 ms; mode switches never add a gap. The full local §12.2 budget (80 ms) was not re-measured end to end.
- **RAM (private bytes, 100 ms PowerShell samples, 20 cycles of 3 s High quality / 3 s Light after a 5 s Light start):** Light baseline 3.0 MB; High quality steady 15.4-17.2 MB in most cycles (up to 27 MB in a few; **about +13 MB** typically); peak 32-35 MB while building. Back on Light the process returns to 3.8-8.6 MB (mean 6.0 over the first ten cycles, 6.2 over the last ten): within about 3 MB of the baseline and plateauing, inside SPEC §12.2's 10 MB. The working set stays at 24-26 MB after switching away (it was 9.6-10.1 MB before) because file-backed pages of the executable, including the 8 MB model, remain resident; an OS trim emptied it while private bytes stayed unchanged. The release binary also carries the compressed 8 MB model.
- **Real microphone (debug build):** `live_high_quality_takes_over_on_the_real_microphone` passes: High quality took over after 1.48 s (debug build time), capture never stalled (249 frames in about 5 s, 505 denoised blocks), no overrun, dropped frame or callback heap operation, and the status events read Light, loading, High quality (1,440 samples), Light.

- **Pending official acceptance (authorized alt account and cooperating official client):** switch Off, Light and High quality during a real call and confirm the other side never hears a gap, with keyboard and background-voice noise reduced under High quality; check CPU and memory in the real app during a call.
